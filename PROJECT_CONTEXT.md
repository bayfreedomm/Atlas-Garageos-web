# Atlas GarageOS Project Context

Atlas GarageOS is a global automotive tools platform intended to grow for many years. **Fitment Studio is the flagship product**, while **My Garage is the shared vehicle-profile and memory backbone** across the platform.

The long-term product family may include tire and wheel fitment, comparison and conversion tools, ownership-cost tools, EV and fuel tools, logs, VIN and recall experiences, a Car Timeline, a Vehicle Passport, and used-car utilities. Milestones should add these as isolated modules instead of turning the initial core into a monolith.

The product direction is modern, premium, fast, and mobile-first. The first milestone deliberately favors mathematical correctness and maintainability over visual polish. SEO is part of the architecture from the beginning rather than a later patch. The product is global and localized: language, unit system, and currency remain independent choices.

Atlas GarageOS should give users correct, explainable information. Pure calculations, explicit assumptions, structured validation errors, and tests are preferred to opaque verdicts. Fitment geometry must never be presented as a real-world safety guarantee.

Modular growth, easy maintenance, static-first delivery, Cloudflare compatibility, and low unnecessary operating cost are lasting constraints. Dependencies and runtime services must earn their place.
