Quick understanding

What matters here

The middle number is a ratio

In 225/45R18, the sidewall is 45% of the 225 mm nominal section width — not 45 mm.

Two sidewalls count

Overall diameter includes the rim plus one sidewall above and one below the wheel.

Real tires vary

Nominal size is a standard label. Actual measured dimensions vary by model, rim width, pressure, load, and manufacturer tolerance.

!
Common mistake

Reading the profile number as millimetres. It is a percentage of the nominal tire width.

How it works

READ THE SIDEWALL

One code. Three physical clues.

The numbers are not three measurements in the same unit. Atlas separates them before doing the geometry.

225/45R18
225Width

Section width in millimetres.

45Sidewall ratio

Sidewall height is 45% of width.

R18Rim

Radial construction on an 18-inch rim.

How the geometry is calculated

Sidewall height is width multiplied by the profile percentage. Two sidewalls plus the rim diameter, converted from inches to millimetres, gives overall diameter.

Diameter and circumference describe rolling geometry. Revolutions show the theoretical number of rotations over one mile or kilometre.

Why the size on the sidewall actually matters

A tire's overall diameter sets how far your car travels per wheel rotation. Fit a noticeably taller or shorter tire and your speedometer starts reading the wrong number — check the real difference with the Speedometer Error Calculator. The same diameter change also shifts your effective gearing, which the Gear Ratio Calculator shows directly.

Keep in mind

These are nominal geometric calculations. Real dimensions vary by tire model, rim width, pressure, load, and manufacturer tolerances.
