Quick understanding

What matters here

Return trips double the distance

Atlas keeps one-way and return-trip distance explicit so the cost is not accidentally undercounted.

Extras can dominate

Tolls, parking, ferries, and other fixed costs can outweigh small fuel-efficiency differences.

Per-person cost is a split, not a saving

Adding passengers changes the share per person, not the vehicle's total trip cost.

!
Common mistake

Comparing two trips using fuel cost alone while ignoring tolls, parking, or a different return distance.

How it works

What is included

Return trips double the entered one-way distance before fuel is calculated. Optional costs are then added, and the complete total is divided by the number of people.

Cost per distance includes the optional trip costs, giving one comparable journey figure.

Keep in mind

Trip costs are estimates based on the numbers you enter. Actual consumption can vary with traffic, weather, load, speed, and driving style.
