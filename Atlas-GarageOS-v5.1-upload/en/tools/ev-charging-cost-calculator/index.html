EV Charging Cost Calculator

See the energy, cost, and time to charge your EV — from your own battery size and electricity price.

Your charging estimate will appear here.

Quick understanding

What matters here

Grid energy is higher than battery energy

Charging losses mean the wall usually supplies more energy than the battery actually stores.

Power is not always constant

Real charging power can taper as state of charge rises, especially during DC fast charging.

Home and public prices can differ sharply

The same energy amount can have very different costs depending on tariff and charging network.

!
Common mistake

Dividing battery energy by the charger label and treating the result as an exact real-world charging time.

!
Important warning

Displayed time is an idealized constant-power estimate. Vehicle limits, charger limits, temperature, battery management, and charging curves can make real sessions longer.

How it works

Why the number is an estimate, not a promise

Charging efficiency accounts for energy lost between the wall and battery. A 90% setting means 10% of grid energy does not reach the battery.

The time estimate assumes the entered charging power stays constant. Real charging can be slower because the vehicle, battery temperature, state of charge, and charging curve can limit power.

Keep in mind

This estimate includes the efficiency you enter. Battery temperature, charge taper, and the vehicle's real charging curve can still change the result.
