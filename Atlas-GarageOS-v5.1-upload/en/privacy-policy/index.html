Privacy policy

Last updated September 2026.

Calculator inputs

No account, login, or email address is required to use the calculators. The values you enter are processed in your browser to produce a result and are not submitted to an Atlas GarageOS application server.

Saved data (My Garage)

Vehicles, measurements, and setups saved to My Garage are stored in your browser's local storage on that device. Clearing browser data can remove them.

Hosting and basic request data

The site is served through Cloudflare. Like other hosting providers, Cloudflare may process standard request information such as IP address, requested URL, browser information, and security signals to deliver and protect the site.

Cookies and advertising

Atlas GarageOS does not currently use advertising cookies or sell personal data. This notice will be updated before advertising or additional tracking technology is introduced.

General audience

Atlas GarageOS is a general-audience automotive utility and is not directed to children under 13.
