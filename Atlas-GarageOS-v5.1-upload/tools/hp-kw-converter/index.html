How it works

Mechanical hp, not PS

One mechanical horsepower is 745.6998715822702 watts. This distinction keeps manufacturer figures and calculations unambiguous.

Keep in mind

This page uses mechanical horsepower. Metric horsepower, often labelled PS, is a different unit and is not silently treated as hp.

Quick understanding

What matters here

Horsepower standards differ

Mechanical horsepower and metric horsepower (PS/CV) are not exactly the same unit.

kW is an SI power unit

Kilowatts express the same physical quantity as horsepower using a different unit scale.

Conversion does not measure output

Changing units cannot tell you whether an advertised engine actually produces that power.

!
Common mistake

Mixing mechanical hp with metric PS/CV as though they were numerically identical.
