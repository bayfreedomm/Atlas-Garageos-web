Quick understanding

What matters here

Consumption drives the estimate

A small change in L/100 km or MPG can materially change total cost on a long trip.

Use a realistic average

City traffic, speed, load, weather, and driving style can make a dashboard average more useful than a brochure figure.

Price is local

The calculator uses the fuel price you enter so it works across currencies and markets.

!
Common mistake

Entering an optimistic manufacturer consumption figure for a trip that will be driven in very different conditions.

How it works

One calculation, two unit systems

Metric mode uses kilometres, litres per 100 kilometres, and price per litre. US mode uses miles, US MPG, and price per US gallon.

The currency is a label only. Atlas does not fetch exchange rates or change the price you enter.

Keep in mind

This is an estimate. Driving style, traffic, weather, load, and actual vehicle consumption can change the result.
