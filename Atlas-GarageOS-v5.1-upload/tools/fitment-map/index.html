Quick understanding

What matters here

It is a geometry map

Each cell represents a wheel width and offset combination relative to the measured baseline.

Targets come from you

Atlas does not invent a universal safe clearance margin.

Neutral is not bad

Without a supplied target, a non-overlap result is shown as checked geometry rather than a green safety verdict.

!
Common mistake

Reading a green or non-overlap geometry state as an approval for road use.

!
Important warning

Dynamic suspension, steering angle, brake shape, tire deformation, alignment, and body movement are not represented by the static map.

How it works

How the map classifies geometry

GREEN means projected clearances are non-negative and any targets you supplied are met. YELLOW means no calculated overlap but at least one supplied target is missed. RED means a projected inner or outer clearance is negative.

Every selected cell exposes the calculated inner movement, outer poke, remaining gaps, and geometry-only explanation.

Keep in mind

Map states describe calculated static geometry from supplied measurements. GREEN is not a road-safety, compatibility, rubbing, or legal verdict.
