How it works

What the conversion means

An Imperial gallon is larger than a US gallon. The same L/100km consumption therefore produces a higher Imperial MPG number.

Lower L/100km means less fuel used, while higher MPG means more distance per gallon. This is a unit conversion, not a claim about real-world efficiency.

Keep in mind

US and Imperial gallons have different volumes. This converter keeps them explicit and never treats the two MPG values as interchangeable.

Quick understanding

What matters here

The scales move in opposite directions

Higher MPG is better, while lower L/100 km is better.

US and Imperial gallons differ

The same MPG number means a different fuel economy depending on which gallon standard is used.

The relationship is reciprocal

MPG and L/100 km do not convert with one fixed multiplier because one measures distance per fuel and the other fuel per distance.

!
Common mistake

Converting MPG without specifying whether the value uses US gallons or Imperial gallons.
